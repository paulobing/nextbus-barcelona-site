Next Bus Barcelona

Next Bus Barcelona is a free iPhone app that shows live public-transport departures near you in Barcelona — bus, metro, tram, FGC and Rodalies — with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It is not yet on the App Store; this page will link to the listing once it goes live.
